Scholaxia Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Scholaxia ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, role (student/teacher), and password (hashed — never stored in plain text).
  • Profile data: subjects, education level, school, and profile photo (optional).
  • Usage data: classes attended, CBT scores, library activity, and AI tutor conversations.
  • Payment data: we use Paystack for payments. We never store full card numbers. Paystack's privacy policy applies to payment data.
  • Device data: browser type, IP address, and device identifiers for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the Scholaxia platform — live classes, CBT practice, AI tutoring, and library access.
  • To personalise your learning experience and recommend content.
  • To process payments and send purchase receipts.
  • To send class notifications and important account updates.
  • To improve the platform and fix bugs.
  • To comply with applicable laws.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service providers: Paystack (payments), Firebase (push notifications), Cloudinary (media), and Render (hosting).
  • Teachers/schools: your name and participation data may be visible to your teacher within a class.
  • Law enforcement: only when required by law.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Scholaxia offers a "Kind" mode for younger learners. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. Parents may contact us to review or delete a child's data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time — see Section 7. Payment records are retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security: HTTPS, bcrypt password hashing, JWT authentication, and Paystack's PCI-DSS-compliant payment infrastructure.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: request a copy of your data.
  • Correction: update your profile at any time from the app.
  • Deletion: request full account deletion — see the deletion page.
  • Portability: export your data on request.
  • Opt out: unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

8. Cookies

We use localStorage and sessionStorage (not third-party cookies) to remember your preferences and session. No advertising trackers.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, email us at scholaxia23@gmail.com or call 0902 166 1674.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app notice.
